Nepal Government Officials Data

This project began with a search for agricultural officials across Nepal and the question of who worked where. I compiled URL lists for municipalities and rural municipalities, then scraped public staff pages linked from those lists to make the available information easier to find in one place.

Download the combined staff data (.xlsx)

This is a point-in-time compilation of information from public source pages, not an official government publication. Records may change; check the source_url in the workbook for the original listing.

How the data was collected

Staff information was collected from the municipality and rural municipality website URLs in the project's source lists. These include staff pages on individual local-government websites, such as Belauri Municipality's Our Team page.

Sources

  • Municipality Association of Nepal (MUAN)
  • Ministry of Federal Affairs and General Administration (MoFAGA)
  • Individual municipality and rural municipality websites listed in the workbook.
